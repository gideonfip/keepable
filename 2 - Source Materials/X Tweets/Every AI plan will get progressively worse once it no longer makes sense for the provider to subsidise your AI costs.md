Every AI plan will get progressively worse once it no longer makes sense for the provider to subsidise your AI costs.

For most non-coders like myself, we don't need the best models, we just need one that gives us good enough outputs.

==Your focus should be on building good Skills with your current Coding Plan== before switching to the cheapest model that can execute the task and give you the output you want.
