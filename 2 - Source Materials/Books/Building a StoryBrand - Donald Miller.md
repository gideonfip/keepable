# Building a StoryBrand

**Author:** Donald Miller  
**Year:** 2017  
**Status:** Reading

## Summary

The StoryBrand framework positions your customer as the hero of the story, and your brand as the guide. Most brands make the mistake of positioning themselves as the hero.

## Key Framework: SB7

1. A **Character** (your customer)
2. Has a **Problem** (villain, external, internal, philosophical)
3. And meets a **Guide** (your brand)
4. Who gives them a **Plan**
5. And calls them to **Action**
6. That helps them avoid **Failure**
7. And ends in **Success**

## Highlights

==The customer is the hero. You are the guide. Never confuse the two.==

==People don't buy the best products. They buy the products they can understand the fastest.==

## My Notes

This framework is directly applicable to landing page copy and email sequences. The "guide" positioning is powerful — it removes the ego from marketing.
